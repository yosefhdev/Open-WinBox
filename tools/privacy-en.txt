OpenWinBox has no account and no analytics of its own. Your settings stay on your PC.

The app connects to the internet only in these cases:

Weather widget (off on a new install)
- Open-Meteo: forecast and city search.
- BigDataCloud: city name for your device location.
- ipapi.co: approximate location from your IP address, when Windows location is off.

Glance widget (off on a new install)
- Microsoft Bing: photo of the day.

Updates
- GitHub: only when you press Buscar actualizaciones.

Each service receives your IP address when the app connects. To avoid these connections, leave those widgets off or turn them off in Configuración > Widgets funcionales.

More information: https://github.com/yosefhdev/Open-WinBox#privacy
